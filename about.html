What this is

Vvynas Vane is an independent, single-developer music player — built first for Android, and reborn here as a web edition so it plays straight from the browser, no install required.

It reads the songs already sitting in a folder you choose on your own device and turns them into a library: playlists, favorites, folders, a recently-played history, monthly recaps, a DJ mode for mixing two decks, and a set of animated backgrounds to play them against. Nothing is uploaded anywhere to make any of that work — see the Privacy Policy for the details.

Built solo

Vvynas Vane is designed, coded, and maintained by one person under the "Vvynas Vane" name — not a company, not a studio. Every screen, animation, and theme in here was hand-built rather than templated, and every piece of feedback actually reaches the person who made it.

  • Local-first: your music library never leaves your device
  • No account, no sign-up, no login wall
  • Actively developed — new themes and features ship over time
